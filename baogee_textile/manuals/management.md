# Management — the picture at a glance

## Where to look
- **HR** home: headcount, present, absent and late today, attendance trend, staff by department.
- **Departments**: headcount by division, department and sub-department, male and female.
- **Attendance**: any period, any department — present, absent, late, early exit.
- **Salary Loans**: outstanding loans, deductions this month against last, disbursements, pending requests.
- **Payroll** home: the last payroll, its total, and the slips.

## Approvals that come to you
- Salary loans outside the policy: only an HR Manager can approve them, with an override reason that is kept on the loan.
- Leave for department heads, and expense claims, as set on each department.

## The loan policy (HR Settings → Salary Loans)
| Rule | Now |
|---|---|
| Who may borrow | Full-time staff with 12 months' service |
| Maximum loan | 3 × average gross pay |
| Maximum instalment | 30% of average net pay |
| Maximum term | 12 months |
| Guarantor | Always; 24 months' service, no loan of their own, backs at most 2 loans |
