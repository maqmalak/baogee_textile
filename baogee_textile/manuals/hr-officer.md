# HR officer — organisation, people, attendance

## Organisation
The organisation has three levels, each a **Department** with a level and an Urdu name:

| Level | Example | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Division | AGER | The top level, under All Departments |
| Department | AGER - Stenter | Belongs to a division |
| Sub Department | Stenter - Helpers | Belongs to a department |

1. **Departments** shows cards or the tree. Each card has the headcount (male / female) of active staff.
2. **New** opens the full-page form: name, Urdu name, parent, code, level, approvers for leave, expenses and shift requests.
3. The headcount tree rolls the numbers up: a division counts everybody in its departments and sub-departments.
4. **Designations** works the same way: title, Urdu name and abbreviation, with the headcount per designation.

## Employees
1. **Employees → New** opens the full-page form:
   - first, middle and last name, the **Urdu name**, CNIC (00000-0000000-0), gender and date of birth;
   - company, department, designation, employee code, date of joining, branch, shift, holiday list;
   - **Attendance Device ID** — the user number on the biometric machine;
   - mobile, emergency contact, basic salary, salary mode and bank details.
2. **Photo**: passport size (35 × 45 mm). Take it with the camera or choose a file.
3. Save opens the employee's page: tabs for overview, job, pay, attendance, leave, advances and documents, and **Connections** (everything linked to the employee) and **Activity** (every change).

*The 448 employees imported from the master sheet carry placeholder gender, date of birth (1990-01-01) and joining date (2026-10-01). Correct them from each profile.*

## Lifecycle
- **Promotions** and **Transfers** change the designation, department or branch from a date and keep the history.
- **Separations** record a leaving employee; the last salary and the full and final settlement follow in Payroll.

## Attendance
- The biometric machines send every punch to **Checkins** through the sync PC at the gate (see *Timekeeper*).
- Shifts: **General** 08:30–16:30, **Shift A** 06:00–14:00, **Shift B** 14:00–22:00, **Shift C** 22:00–06:00. Late entry and early exit are marked after a **30-minute grace**. Change grace periods in **HR Setup**.
- **Attendance** shows present, absent, late and on leave for any period, by department.
- Missing punches: the employee files an **Attendance Requests** entry, or HR marks attendance with the **Employee Attendance Tool**.

## Leave
- **Holiday Lists**: Sundays and public holidays for the year, assigned to the company.
- **Leave Allocations** give each employee their days per leave type; **Leave Applications** are approved by the department's leave approver.

## Advances and loans
- **Employee Advances**: a cash advance (recovered in full from the next salary) or a salary loan (equal monthly instalments). See *Cash advances and salary loans*.
- **Salary Loans** shows everything outstanding, this month's deductions and the loan ledger.
