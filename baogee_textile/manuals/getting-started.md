# Getting started

## Signing in
- Open the HR & Payroll app in the browser and sign in with your user and password from HR.
- The **Desktop** shows the apps you can use: **HR**, **Payroll**, **Help & Guides** and your account.
- The company chooser in the header is **Bao Gee Textile**. Every list and report follows it.

## Finding your way
- Each app has a sidebar grouped by the work: in HR — Employees, Lifecycle, Shifts & Attendance, Leave, Claims & Extras; in Payroll — Run payroll, Overtime, Structures, Tax.
- The app's home page shows the same pages as big icons, and the figures for today.
- **Search** (Ctrl K) finds any page, employee or document by name.
- A page with a small arrow icon opens in the ERPNext desk in a new tab (a few tools live only there).

## Lists and forms
- Lists can be shown as cards, a table or a board; filter by status, search, and sort by any column.
- **New** (the coloured button) opens a full-page form. Required fields have a red star; **Save** keeps a draft.
- Documents that move money or attendance (advances, salary slips, leave) are **approved** (submitted) and then can only be cancelled, not edited.

## Urdu names
- Departments, designations and employees have an **Urdu name** field. Type with the normal English keyboard: letters turn into Urdu by sound (a → ا, b → ب, p → پ, Shift + T → ٹ, Shift + R → ڑ).
- A real Urdu keyboard (Windows or Android) works too.
