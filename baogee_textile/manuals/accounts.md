# Accounts — advances, pay-outs and payroll posting

## Paying an advance or a loan
1. HR approves the advance or salary loan in **Employee Advances**.
2. Open it: the bottom bar shows **Pay out** with the mode (Cash, Cheque, Wire Transfer, Bank Draft).
3. Pay out makes a submitted **Payment Entry** from the cash or bank account to the employee's advance account.
4. For a salary loan the monthly instalments are set up at once (from the first deduction month); for a cash advance press **Recover from next salary**.

## How recovery reaches the books
- The instalment is a deduction on the salary slip (**Loan Recovery** or **Advance Recovery**).
- When the payroll is posted, the deduction credits **Employee Advances** for that employee, and the advance's balance goes down.
- A cash advance shows **Returned** when fully recovered.

## Accounts the payroll uses
| Account | Type | Used for |
|---|---|---|
| Employee Advances | Receivable | Advances and loans to employees |
| Payroll Payable | Payable | Net salaries until paid |
| Salary | Expense | Earnings |
| Cash / bank | Cash / Bank | Pay-outs and salary payment |

## Checks each month
- **Salary Loans** → loan ledger: disbursed, deducted from salary, repaid, balance per employee.
- Advances older than their term with nothing scheduled are flagged as **in default** in the eligibility check.
